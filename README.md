# Foot & Ellis-Smith — Local Sites Lab concept

Independent redesign concept for **Foot & Ellis-Smith**, accountants on Ongar High Street (established 1970).

- **Official site:** https://www.foot-ellis-smith.co.uk/
- **This repo:** sample concept by Local Sites Lab — not live, not approved by the business
- **Design brief:** [research/DESIGN_BRIEF.md](research/DESIGN_BRIEF.md)
- **Source log:** [research/SOURCE_LOG.md](research/SOURCE_LOG.md)
