# Design brief — Foot & Ellis-Smith (Local Sites Lab concept)

## Business
- **Name:** Foot & Ellis-Smith
- **Trade:** Accountants / tax advisers / payroll & support (professional services)
- **Place:** 47B High Street, Ongar, Essex CM5 9DT
- **Primary customer job:** Arrange a free no-obligation meeting via call, email or visit — not emergency call-out.
- **Source site:** https://www.foot-ellis-smith.co.uk/

## Target customer
Local SME owners, sole traders/partnerships, personal tax clients, and (separately) direct-payment service users needing PA payroll. Sceptical High Street buyers need: longevity, clear service list, named director context, and frictionless call/email/visit.

## Why this layout differs from other Local Sites Lab concepts
Other Harlow-area LSL concepts lean on trade patterns (emergency command heroes, urgency tiles, sticky call docks, pathway switchers for electricians/drainage). This concept uses a **Practice Desk Consultative** architecture:

1. Quiet navy utility bar (phone/email) + cream header with official logo (light plate for dark wordmark contrast)
2. Full-bleed split hero — solid navy copy panel **beside** real practice photo (no scrim, no text-over-image)
3. Trust band of verified practice facts (1970, High Street, free meeting, small-business focus) — **no invented star ratings**
4. Three-step meeting pathway (call/email → free meeting → ongoing advice)
5. Two-by-two service clarity matrix + compliance panel
6. Practice story with director notes and stated values
7. Visit High Street conversion panel

Section order, typography (serif headlines), and conversion path are purpose-built for accountants.

## Visual direction
- Logo-led navy `#16284D` / `#213D75` on cream `#F7F3EA`
- Warm copper accent `#C45C26` for primary meeting CTAs on dark panels
- No box-shadows; elevation via borders and background contrast
- Full-width heroes; solid copy panels; high contrast; min 44px targets
